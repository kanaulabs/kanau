For a bug report, please include the fixture model and mode, the output route (Art-Net / sACN / CRMX), and the app version, which is shown at the bottom of Settings.

Requirements

  • iOS 17 or later. iPhone is portrait; iPad is landscape.
  • Over the network (Art-Net / sACN) — the iPhone and the lighting nodes must be on the same network. The app asks for Local Network permission the first time.
  • Over Bluetooth (CRMX) — the app asks for Bluetooth permission the first time.

If something is not working

The troubleshooting chapter of the manual is arranged by symptom. These are the three we are asked about most.

No light

The output section in Settings shows Destination, Medium, Clock and Touch → wire. If those are moving, the app is sending, and the problem is further down the line.

sACN multicast is refused

iOS can refuse multicast sending. When the screen says so, switch to unicast and enter the node's address.

A fixture is missing from the library

Send us the manufacturer and model name. You can also build the definition yourself in the app, or import a GDTF file.
