Privacy Policy

Kanau

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Kanau Labs does not collect, receive, or store any information about you. There are no Kanau accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data the app creates

Your patch, scenes, settings, and MIDI assignments stay on your device. Files you export go wherever you choose to put them.

What the app connects to

  • Local network — Art-Net and sACN to lighting nodes on the same network, sessions between your own devices, and receiving DMX in LAMP MODE
  • Bluetooth — DMX to CRMX transmitters
  • Camera — only to read a session's QR code when you join one. Nothing is recorded or sent
  • The internet — only when you use GDTF Share. You sign in with your own GDTF Share account, and the app connects to gdtf-share.com to list and download fixture definitions. Your sign-in goes to GDTF Share only; if you choose to have it remembered, it is kept in this device's keychain. Kanau Labs runs no server and receives nothing. Your GDTF Share account is covered by GDTF Share's own terms

The local network and Bluetooth go to equipment on your own site.

Third parties

Kanau contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, or ad networks, and shares nothing with anyone. The only outside service it talks to is GDTF Share, and only when you sign in to it.

Children's privacy

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date on this page is updated.
