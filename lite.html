Kanau Lite

As of Kanau Lite 1.0 (3)

Kanau Lite is a free app that turns an iPhone or iPad into one light. It receives DMX sent from a desk over Art-Net or sACN, and drives the color and brightness of the screen, the back light and the vibration. It is Kanau's LITE MODE as an app of its own. There is no account to create and there are no ads.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS 17 or later.
  • A desk that sends Art-Net or sACN. Kanau or any other desk.
  • The same network as the desk, over Wi-Fi or a wired connection. The first time you open the app, it asks for Local Network access. Allow it.

Getting started

  1. Open Kanau Lite. The icon and the name show for a moment. Tap to skip them. They also go away as soon as DMX arrives.
  2. A black screen shows the numbers this device listens on, in large type. This is the address screen. Tap anywhere on it to open Settings.
  3. Choose Art-Net or sACN. Then set Mode, Universe and Address.
  4. Tap × at the top left to close Settings.
  5. From the desk, send DMX to that universe and address. When it arrives, the screen becomes the light.

The address screen

While no DMX is arriving, the black screen shows three lines. With many devices side by side, you can read each number from a distance.

  • The universe and address, in amber, for example U1 · 001.
  • The mode, for example SCREEN RGB 10ch.
  • The network, this device's IP address and the protocol it listens for, for example Wi-Fi 192.168.1.24 · Art-Net. With no network, it shows No network.

If no DMX arrives for 6 seconds while the light is on, the address screen comes back. The back light and the vibration stop too.

Settings

Type a number for Universe and Address, or use − and +. The amber line below shows the addresses used by the screen, the back light and the vibration, for example Screen 001–008 · Back Light 009 · Vibration 010.

Below that is Channels. Each time you open Settings, it starts folded and shows only the number of channels, for example 10ch. Tap the heading to open the list of the current mode's channels, with their address, name and what the values mean. The two lines on patching on Kanau and on other desks show even while it is folded. A row for a feature this device does not have (the back light or the vibration) is dimmed and starts with Not on this device. That row still uses its address, so count it on the desk too.

At the end of Settings are the Kanau Lite version, Made by Kanau Labs and a link to kanaulabs.app. Settings are saved and are still there the next time you open the app.

While the light is on

The screen shows the color it receives. While Kanau Lite is open, the screen stays at full brightness and does not lock automatically.

To open Settings while the light is on, slide the faint switch at the top right all the way to the right, then tap the × that appears. The × goes away after 3 seconds.

Kanau Lite receives DMX only while the app is open. If you go to the Home Screen or lock the device, it stops receiving, stops the back light and the vibration, and sets the screen brightness back to what it was. Open it again to receive again.

The HUE SAT modes have Hue (0–360°) and Saturation instead of Red, Green and Blue. In the CELLS modes, Screen Dimmer is followed by Dimmer, CCT, G/M, Crossfade and the color for each part of the screen. Parts are numbered from the top left. With 8 parts, a portrait screen has 2 columns and 4 rows, and a landscape screen has 4 columns and 2 rows.

Crossfade sets the mix of white and color: 0 is white and 255 is color. The white comes from CCT and G/M. While Crossfade is at 0, raising Red, Green or Blue leaves the screen white. Screen Dimmer dims the screen only and does not affect the back light. Setting Function to 128 or higher selects Proximity, which uses the device's proximity sensor.

Patching on a desk

On Kanau, patch this device as Kanau Screen with the same mode (chapter 03 of the manual).

On other desks, make a fixture with the channels in the same order as Channels in Settings, and patch it at the same address. Every channel is 8-bit.

Over Art-Net, the app answers node discovery (ArtPoll) with a name such as Kanau Screen · U1 · 001. On a desk that can list nodes, look for this name, with its universe and address.

How it differs from LITE MODE in Kanau

Kanau Lite receives the same way and has the same modes as LITE MODE in Kanau (chapter 02 of the manual). Three things are different.

  • Before DMX arrives, LITE MODE shows WAITING. Kanau Lite shows the address screen.
  • When DMX stops, LITE MODE keeps the last color. Kanau Lite goes back to the address screen after 6 seconds.
  • The × from the top-right switch leaves LITE MODE in Kanau. In Kanau Lite, it opens Settings.
