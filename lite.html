Kanau Lite

As of Kanau Lite 1.0 (6)

Kanau Lite is a free app that turns an iPhone or iPad into one light. It receives DMX sent from a desk over Art-Net or sACN, and drives the color and brightness of the screen, the back light and the vibration. In the FX & MEDIA and ULTIMATE modes, the screen can also show moving patterns and your own images and videos. There is no account to create and there are no ads.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS 17 or later.
  • A desk that sends Art-Net or sACN. Kanau or any other desk.
  • The same network as the desk, over Wi-Fi or a wired connection. The first time you open the app, it asks for Local Network access. Allow it.

Getting started

  1. Open Kanau Lite. The icon and the name show for a moment. Tap to skip them.
  2. Settings opens. It comes first each time Kanau Lite opens.
  3. Choose a Mode, then Art-Net or sACN, and set Universe and Address.
  4. Tap START at the bottom.
  5. From the desk, send DMX to that universe and address. When it arrives, the screen becomes the light.

Until you tap START, the screen does not light up, even if DMX arrives.

The address screen

After START, while no DMX is arriving, a black screen shows three lines in large type. This is the address screen. With many devices side by side, you can read each number from a distance.

  • The universe and address, in amber, for example U1 · 001.
  • The mode, for example 2: SCREEN RGB 9ch.
  • The network, this device's IP address and the protocol it listens for, for example Wi-Fi 192.168.1.24 · Art-Net. With no network, it shows No network.

Tap the address screen to open Settings. If no DMX arrives for 6 seconds while the light is on, the address screen comes back. The back light and the vibration stop too.

Type a number for Universe and Address, or use − and +. The amber line below them shows the addresses used by the screen, the back light and the vibration, for example Screen 001–007 · Back Light 008 · Vibration 009.

Channels comes next. Each time you open Settings, it starts folded and shows only the number of channels, for example 9ch. Tap the heading to open the list of the current mode's channels, with their address, name and what the values mean. The two lines on patching on Kanau and on other desks show even while it is folded. A row for a feature this device does not have (the back light or the vibration) is dimmed and starts with Not on this device. That row still uses its address, so count it on the desk too.

At the end of Settings are the Kanau Lite version, Made by Kanau Labs, a link to kanaulabs.app, Privacy Policy, Contact Support, the Art-Net credit and the copyright. Settings are saved and are still there the next time you open the app.

While the light is on

The screen shows what it receives. While Kanau Lite is open, the screen stays at full brightness and does not lock automatically.

To open Settings while the light is on, slide the faint switch at the top right all the way to the right, then tap the × that appears. The × goes away after 3 seconds. Settings opened this way closes with × at the top left.

Kanau Lite receives DMX only while the app is open. If you go to the Home Screen or lock the device, it stops receiving, stops the back light and the vibration, and sets the screen brightness back to what it was. Open it again to receive again.

The HUE SAT modes have Hue (0–360°) and Saturation instead of Red, Green and Blue. In the CELLS and STRIPS modes, Screen Dimmer is followed by Dimmer, CCT, G/M, Crossfade and the color for each part of the screen. CELLS are numbered from the top left. With 8 cells, a portrait screen has 2 columns and 4 rows, and a landscape screen has 4 columns and 2 rows. STRIPS are strips of equal width placed along the long side of the screen. A portrait screen has horizontal strips numbered from top to bottom, and a landscape screen has vertical strips numbered from left to right. A square screen is split from top to bottom.

Crossfade sets the mix of white and color: 0 is white and 255 is color. The white comes from CCT and G/M. While Crossfade is at 0, raising Red, Green or Blue leaves the screen white. Screen Dimmer dims the screen only and does not affect the back light.

The screen is built up from black: first the image or video chosen by Media, then the pattern chosen by Content on top of it. Mix sets how much of each you see: 0 shows only the image or video, and 255 shows only the pattern. When Media is 0, or the number holds nothing, only the pattern shows.

SOLID, the first Content, fills the screen with the color. At the Home values, FX & MEDIA gives the same light as SCREEN RGB. The other seven are patterns that move. Speed sets how fast they move, and Amount and Size change how each pattern looks. Each pattern has colors of its own, so it looks finished even when you set no color. Crossfade moves a pattern from its own colors at 0 to the color of Red, Green and Blue at 255. When Color Macro is not 0, the macro color is used instead. From 16, each 16 values pick the next color: 3200 K, 4300 K, 5600 K, Red, Orange, Amber, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Sky Blue, Blue, Deep Blue, Lavender, Magenta and Pink.

Images and videos keep their own colors. Color Temp shifts them warmer or cooler, and Screen Dimmer dims them. Videos play without sound and start again from the beginning when they end. Playback plays or pauses the video. Restart sends it back to the beginning once, at the moment the value enters 171–255.

COOKIE and ULTIMATE

SCREEN COOKIE splits the screen into irregular, dappled areas. Each area has the color of one of the 8 cells, so the color of one cell shows in several places on the screen. Some areas stay black whatever the cells are, so there is shadow between the light even with all 8 cells lit. For more shadow, set some of the cells to black on the desk.

16: SCREEN COOKIE RGB 62ch starts with Screen Dimmer, then Shape, Soft and Drift. Next come the 8 cells, each with Dimmer, CCT, G/M, Crossfade, Red, Green and Blue, as in the CELLS modes. Back Light and Vibration come last. 17: SCREEN COOKIE HUE SAT 54ch has Hue and Saturation in each cell instead of Red, Green and Blue.

#NameValues
2Shape0–255 Pattern · Home 0
3Soft0 Sharp → 255 Soft · Home 0

Shape chooses the pattern. The pattern changes smoothly as the value moves, so a fade on the desk turns one pattern into the next. Soft blurs the edges between the areas: at 0 they are sharp. Drift sets how fast the pattern flows: at 0 it stands still.

The cells show only while Content is SOLID and Media is 0. Cells then chooses how the screen is split. SCREEN fills the whole screen with the color of cell 1. While a pattern, an image or a video shows (Content is not SOLID, or Media is not 0), the screen is one, as in FX & MEDIA, and the pattern takes its color and Crossfade from cell 1. Media counts by its value: a value other than 0 makes the screen one, even if that number holds nothing. Color Macro works only while the screen is one. While the screen is split, it has no effect. When Cells splits the screen into fewer than 8 parts, the channels of the other cells have no effect, but still use their addresses.

Media

The shelf holds images and videos on this device under the numbers 1 to 51. Open it from Media in Settings. It is there in every mode, so you can fill it before you switch to FX & MEDIA or ULTIMATE.

  • Tap an empty number to add an image or a video from Photos or from Files.
  • Tap a number in use to replace what it holds, move it to another number, or delete it. If the other number is in use, the two swap.

On the desk, the Media channel picks the number: 1–5 is 1, 6–10 is 2, and so on, up to 251–255 for 51. The files stay on this device and are not sent anywhere.

Patching on a desk

On Kanau, patch this device as Kanau Lite with the same mode (chapter 03 of the manual). Kanau 1.0 (372) and later have all 19 modes under Kanau Lite.

On other desks, make a fixture with the channels in the same order as Channels in Settings, and patch it at the same address. Every channel is 8-bit.

Over Art-Net, the app answers node discovery (ArtPoll) with a name such as Kanau Lite · U1 · 001. On a desk that can list nodes, look for this name, with its universe and address.

How it differs from LITE MODE in Kanau

Kanau Lite receives the same way as LITE MODE in Kanau (chapter 02 of the manual). As of Kanau 1.0 (375), three things are different.

  • Before DMX arrives, LITE MODE shows WAITING. Kanau Lite shows the address screen.
  • When DMX stops, LITE MODE keeps the last color. Kanau Lite goes back to the address screen after 6 seconds.
  • The × from the top-right switch leaves LITE MODE in Kanau. In Kanau Lite, it opens Settings.
